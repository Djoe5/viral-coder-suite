## 💰 Monetization Guide: How to Siphon Income

### 1. High-Ticket Freelance Pitching ($500 - $1,500 / client)
* Run `python money-makers/01-lead-scraper-audit/scraper.py`.
* It scans local businesses with 4.5+ star reviews that **lack a website** or have **broken mobile/SSL**.
* Open `money-makers/01-lead-scraper-audit/audit_report.html` to view the personalized outreach scripts.
* Send the 2-minute video walkthrough pitch. Close 1-2 clients per month for simple modern web redesigns.

### 2. Micro-SaaS Subscription ($9 - $19 / month)
* The **AI ATS Resume Optimizer** in `money-makers/02-ai-ats-optimizer/index.html` can be deployed in 60 seconds to Vercel, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages.
* Add a simple Stripe payment link or Lemon Squeezy checkout to charge for full resume rewrites or PDF downloads.

### 3. ManyChat Audience & Lead Gen Engine
* Run `python money-makers/03-viral-content-engine/generator.py`.
* Follow the exact 30-second reel scripts in `viral_packet.md`.
* Use the ManyChat call-to-action (*"Comment 'CODE' to get the link"*) to grow a warm developer/client audience on Instagram and LinkedIn on autopilot.
